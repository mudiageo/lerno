# Official Rules — section-by-section digest

Full title: _#BuildHybridAI 2026 Official Eligibility and Rules_. Source: https://firebasehybridaihackathon.devpost.com/rules, accessed 28 Nov 2026. Quoted text below is verbatim where marked.

## Preamble

> "Google will share specific challenges set forth in these Rules as well as in the Devpost page for this Contest, and participants must develop solutions to one of the challenges."

No separate named challenge tracks are actually listed on either page. The only challenge is the hybrid-app brief. Platform categories are Web/Chrome, Android, iOS.

## 1. Binding agreement

Submission constitutes agreement to the Rules; they form a binding legal agreement between entrant and Google.

## 2. Sponsor & Administrator

Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheater Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043. Devpost, Inc., 250 Broadway, 24th Floor, New York, NY 10007.

## 3. Eligibility

Entrant must: be above the age of majority where they live at entry (20+ in Taiwan); not reside in, nor have an organisation domiciled in, Brazil, Quebec, Crimea, Russia, Italy, Syria, Sudan, Belarus, Cuba, Iran, North Korea or any OFAC-designated country; not be under US export controls or sanctions; **have had internet access as of 11 Nov 2026**.

Ineligible: Google/Devpost employees, interns, contractors, office-holders, affiliates, their immediate families and households; government-agency employees; anyone creating a real or apparent conflict of interest. Employer entry requires entity knowledge, consent to prizes and no work-for-hire breach. Google may verify eligibility and adjudicate disputes at any time.

## 4. Contest period

Opens 11 Nov 2026, 1:30 PM PT; closes 30 Nov 2026, 2:00 PM PT. Judging 10 Dec 9:00 AM PT → 16 Dec 2:00 PM PT. Winners on or around 21 Dec, 12:00 PM PT. Entrants handle timezone conversion.

## 5. How to enter

Devpost account required. Entry consents to collection of personal data for running and publicising the contest. All required fields on the "Enter a Submission" page must be completed during the Contest Period.

Quota: all required tools, including Firebase AI Logic and Gemini Nano 4, are free via the free tier. Billing-required models need the Quota Form, no purchase required. An account lasts four days from issue, one per entrant per project per sprint, only for an individual or team Representative. Sprint 1 by 11 Nov, Sprint 2 by 18 Nov, Sprint 3 (final) by 25 Nov 10:00 AM PT, issued 27 Nov. All closed.

## 6. Submission Requirements

**What to create:** "Build a Gen AI application using Firebase AI Logic along with supported firebase products such as: AppCheck, RemoteConfig, Firestore and Authentication." At least one platform: Web/Chrome (Gemini Nano 4 via Firebase AI Logic, web app or Chrome extension), Android (Gemini Nano 4 via Firebase AI Logic), iOS (Apple Foundation Models API for local execution via Firebase AI Logic). "Firebase AI Logic requires App Check; debug tokens are accepted in submissions."

**Team:** individual, team or organisation. Teams must contain only eligible individuals, all added as Devpost project members, with one authorised Representative.

**Functionality:** "capable of being successfully installed and run consistently on the platform for which it is intended, and must function as depicted in the video and/or text description."

**New & existing projects:** existing apps eligible only if "significantly updated after the start of the Hackathon Submission Period." Tools, frameworks, starter templates and AI coding assistants allowed, "but must disclose any other pre-existing code or work incorporated into the Project." A summary of what was built during the submission period is mandatory.

**Third-party integrations:** entrant must be authorised to use every SDK, API and dataset under its terms.

**Testing:** provide a working link; if private, include login credentials. Project "must be available free of charge and without any restriction" to Sponsor, Administrator and Judges until the Judging Period ends. Judges need not test and may judge on description, images and video alone. "Entrants must not include production API keys, live Google Cloud project secrets, confidential commercial data, or personally identifiable information (PII) within their repository, submission form, or testing instructions." Proprietary hardware can be requisitioned.

**Language:** app must support English; all materials in English or translated.

**Multiple submissions:** allowed, but each must be unique and substantially different.

**Ownership and IP:** original work, solely owned, no violation of copyright, trademark, patent, contract or privacy rights. Third-party technical assistance allowed only if components remain entrant's work product and entrant owns all rights. Open source allowed if licensed and the submission "creates software that enhances and builds upon the features and functionality included in the underlying open source product" — repackaging alone is insufficient.

**Financial or preferential support:** nothing developed from a project funded, contracted or commercially licensed by Google or Devpost before the submission period ends.

**The entry checklist:**

- select at least one platform; Sponsor may reassign categories;
- for an existing app with public usage: describe the core value the existing app brings to the new project; usage numbers **optional**;
- description covers Firebase AI Logic usage, features and functionality, value proposition;
- public GitHub/GitLab repo with all source, assets and run instructions, and it "must demonstrate the use of Firebase AI Logic at runtime in your code — imported and actually called (a library import, an app/backend entry point, or a loaded agent/flow/MCP config), **not just named in the README**";
- "Must have an open source license. The repository must be public and open source by including an open source license file. This license should be detectable and visible at the top of the repository page (in the About section). Although Apache is preferred, any open source license can be used."
- demo video showing the project working, ≤3 minutes (only first 3 evaluated), public on YouTube, linked on the form, English or subtitled. Hosted URL "strongly encouraged";
- Google IDEs encouraged, any tool allowed "provided the implementation is Firebase AI Logic";
- content bans: no derogatory, offensive, threatening, defamatory, discriminatory or indecent material; nothing unlawful; no third-party advertising, slogans, logos or trademarks implying sponsorship; nothing violating third-party publicity, privacy or IP rights.

## 7. Modifications

Drafts may be saved before submission. After the deadline the Submission is locked, though the Devpost portfolio project may still be updated. Narrow post-deadline fixes may be permitted for infringement, PII or inappropriate content. "Commits pushed to the code repository after the Submission Period ends will not be evaluated."

## 8. Judging

Stage One: pass/fail — all requirements present, reasonably addresses a Challenge, reasonably applies the requirements.

Stage Two: 1–5 scores averaged across four equally weighted criteria: **Technological Implementation** (build quality and effective use of hybrid inference), **Design** (complete coherent product, not a technical proof of concept), **Potential Impact** (credible specific case for a real problem and audience, addressed by what is demonstrated), **Quality of the Idea** (creative, non-obvious use of Firebase AI Logic, genuine understanding of the problem space).

Highest score per category wins the category; highest across all wins Grand Prize. Ties break in criterion order then judges' vote. Determinations final. Potential winners must respond within three business days and return affidavits within ten business days.

## 9. Prizes

Grand $10,000 (interview, featured blog post, social mention); 1st runner-up $7,000; 2nd runner-up $4,000; Best iOS, Best Android, Best Web/Chrome $3,000 each. One project can win one grand prize and one Best-of. Non-cash recognition has ARV $0. Note: Rules omit an interview for 1st runner-up; overview page includes one.
