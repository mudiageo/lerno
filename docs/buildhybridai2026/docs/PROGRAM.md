# Program brief — #BuildHybridAI 2026

Sponsor: Google LLC. Administrator: Devpost, Inc. Prize pool: $30,000 cash.

| Contest opens       | 11 Nov 2026, 1:30 PM PT                         |

A started Devpost draft is not a submission. Submit early, keep editing until the deadline. YouTube processing can take hours. Commits pushed after the deadline are not evaluated.

## Eligibility

- Above the age of majority where you live (20+ in Taiwan), at time of entry.
- Nigeria is eligible. Excluded: Brazil, Italy, Quebec, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, Syria, North Korea, Sudan, Belarus, Russia, and OFAC-sanctioned countries.
- Internet access as of 11 Nov 2026.
- No Google/Devpost employees, contractors, affiliates, their families/households; no government-agency employees; no conflicts of interest.
- Entrant is **solo: Mudia**, Nigeria.

## What to build

A generative-AI app using **Firebase AI Logic**, with hybrid inference: on-device Gemini Nano 4 where available, cloud fallback otherwise. App Check is required (debug tokens accepted by the contest, but never committed to the public repo).

Eligible platforms: Web/Chrome (web app or Chrome extension) with Gemini Nano 4 via Firebase AI Logic; Android with Gemini Nano 4 via Firebase AI Logic; iOS with Apple Foundation Models via Firebase AI Logic. Multi-platform is preferred for the Grand Prize; one strong platform can win. Native macOS/Windows companions do not qualify.

Other Firebase products (App Check, Remote Config, Firestore, Authentication) are encouraged but optional. All AI inference must go through Firebase AI Logic — no direct model APIs.

## Submission requirements

- Select at least one platform category.
- Public GitHub/GitLab repo with all source, assets and run instructions; an open-source licence file visible in the repo's About section (Apache preferred); Firebase AI Logic **imported and actually called at runtime**, not just named in the README.
- Text description: how Firebase AI Logic was used, features and functionality, value proposition.
- Public YouTube demo video, ≤3 minutes (only first 3 evaluated), English or subtitled, showing the project working on the target platform.
- Working demo URL strongly encouraged; free and unrestricted through 16 Dec; credentials in testing instructions if private.
- No production API keys, live Google Cloud secrets, confidential data or PII in repo, form or instructions.
- App must support English; all materials in English or translated.
- IP must be original and solely owned; no financial or preferential support from Google or Devpost.

**Existing app rules (apply to Lerno):** significantly updated after 11 Nov 2026; disclose pre-existing code; provide a summary of what was built during the contest; describe the core value the existing app brings to the new project (usage numbers optional).

## Judging

Stage 1: pass/fail on all requirements and a reasonable attempt at the challenge.

Stage 2: four equally weighted 1–5 criteria, averaged:

1. **Technological Implementation** — how well built, how effectively hybrid inference is used.
2. **Design** — complete, coherent product, not a proof of concept.
3. **Potential Impact** — credible, specific case for a real problem and audience.
4. **Quality of the Idea** — creative, non-obvious use of Firebase AI Logic, genuine understanding of the problem space.

Ties break in criterion order, then judges vote. One project can win one grand prize and one Best-of category.

## Prizes

Grand $10,000 (interview, blog post, social mention); 1st runner-up $7,000; 2nd runner-up $4,000; Best iOS / Best Android / Best Web-Chrome $3,000 each. Winners complete affidavits within 10 business days and submit W-9 (US) or W-8BEN (non-US).

## Quota

Text inference is free within limits. Sprint 3 (final) quota requests closed 25 Nov 2026; accounts issued 27 Nov and valid four days. No further quota windows. Do not build around billing-required models.
